A mutex, or mutual exclusion lock, is a synchronization primitive used to control access to a shared resource by multiple threads. It ensures that only one thread can access the resource at a time, preventing race conditions. You would use a mutex when you need to protect a shared resource from concurrent access, such as a shared variable, a file, or a database connection.
